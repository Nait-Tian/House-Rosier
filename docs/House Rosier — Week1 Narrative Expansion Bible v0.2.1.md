# House Rosier --- Week1 Narrative Expansion Bible v0.2.1

## Overview

This update expands Week 1 from a world introduction chapter into the
first emotional and political chapter of House Rosier.

The central theme:

> The kingdom asks whether the person who awakened is still Nait Rosier.
> The player decides what that answer means.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Core Premise: The Rosier Heir Rumor

During Nait Rosier's two months of coma, rumors spread:

-   The sole heir of House Rosier may not recover.
-   The succession may become unstable.
-   Political opponents begin watching the family.

When the player awakens, the question is not only recovery.

The real question is:

> Is the person who returned still someone worthy of standing as
> Rosier's heir?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Rosecourt Spring Garden Party

## Public Meaning

A traditional spring flower gathering.

Official purposes:

-   celebrate the blooming roses;
-   announce the heir's recovery;
-   reconnect with noble society.

## Hidden Meaning

Everyone understands this is also a test.

Guests observe:

-   physical recovery;
-   social ability;
-   leadership potential;
-   whether House Rosier remains stable.

The garden party is beautiful on the surface, but political underneath.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Three Independent Systems

## Identity Axis

Question:

> Who am I?

### Otherworlder

The player believes their consciousness arrived through a magical
accident.

Themes:

-   displacement;
-   borrowed life;
-   choosing whether to accept this world.

### Original Nait

The player believes they are Nait Rosier with damaged memories.

Themes:

-   recovery;
-   reconciliation with the past.

### Self-Defined

The player believes identity is created by the present.

Themes:

-   freedom;
-   becoming someone new.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Rosier Family Affinity

Separate from identity.

Question:

> What does House Rosier mean to me?

Possible directions:

-   Responsibility
-   Distance
-   Balance

Identity does not decide this.

A player can be an Otherworlder and still choose to protect Rosier.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Romance Affinity

Romance is based on actions, not identity.

Characters fall in love with the person revealed through choices.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Romance Foundations

## Damien Aurelian

Theme:

**Choosing responsibility despite uncertainty**

Damien is not attracted only to the Rosier heir.

He is attracted to someone who could leave, but chooses to stand.

Key moments:

-   handling political pressure;
-   protecting family dignity;
-   making decisions without relying on old memories.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Alaric Varenne

Theme:

**Accepting the person in front of him**

Alaric is interested in the difference between memory and identity.

He does not need proof that the player is the old Nait.

Key moments:

-   discussions about memory;
-   accepting uncertainty;
-   discovering the player's true thoughts.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Roderic Wycliffe

Theme:

**Who you are under pressure**

Roderic cares about actions more than names.

Key moments:

-   facing insults;
-   handling danger;
-   choosing honesty or humor.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Lucien & Elise Family Route

Theme:

**Being loved beyond identity**

Their question:

> Are you still our child?

This route explores:

-   rebuilding trust;
-   accepting family;
-   defining what Rosier means.

Possible endings:

-   Otherworlder who chooses to become Rosier.
-   Original Nait returning to their life.
-   A person who remains themselves while being accepted by family.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Week 1 Expanded Flow

## Day 4 --- Rumors Reach Rosecourt

The player learns that their awakening changed the political situation.

## Day 5 --- Preparation

Family and companions prepare the player for society.

## Day 6 --- First Impressions

Three separate encounters:

-   Damien: responsibility.
-   Alaric: identity.
-   Roderic: courage.

## Day 7 --- Rosecourt Spring Garden Party

The player faces society for the first time.

This is not a route lock.

It is the first statement of who the player chooses to become.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Design Principle

Identity answers:

> Who am I?

Romance answers:

> Who do they love?

Family answers:

> Where do I belong?

These systems should interact, but never replace each other.
