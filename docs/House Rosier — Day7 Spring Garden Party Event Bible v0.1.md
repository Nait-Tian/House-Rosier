# House Rosier — Day7 Spring Garden Party Event Bible v0.1

## Dramatic purpose

Day7 is the Week1 climax. The official event is Rosecourt's Spring Garden Party. Everyone understands the unofficial purpose: verify that House Rosier's sole heir is physically capable, mentally capable, and still able to act in public.

The sequence is intentionally three tests rather than four:

1. an ally's sincere concern about Nait's health;
2. a political opponent's trap concerning memory loss;
3. an accidental household crisis involving a maid and Countess Bellacourt.

Between the first and second tests, the player may actively seek Damien, Alaric, Roderic, or Lucien & Elise. This is interest, not route lock.

The final ten minutes offer Damien, Alaric, Roderic, family, or rest. Multiple romance first-stirring flags may trigger. Parallel attraction is intentional and is not a harem-route commitment.

## Social shift

The success signal is not applause. After the household accident is handled, guests begin asking Nait for opinions about politics and estate matters instead of asking only about health.

## Family payoff

Lucien's conclusion is not “you acted like the old Nait,” but “you acted like yourself.” Elise continues to prioritize Nait's physical condition over public performance.

## Callback targets

Day7 reads earlier behavior such as honesty about uncertainty, humor under pressure, recovery discipline, family distance/support, evidence-first reasoning, boundary setting, old-Nait accountability, and shared action with Roderic.

## First-stirring philosophy

The love interests are attracted to how the player thinks and acts, not to the player's metaphysical identity conclusion. Multiple characters can become interested in the same playthrough for different reasons. Romantic commitment remains a later system.
